# GR Math Companion (Three Rungs)

Every topic: **A** words/pictures → **B** algebra → **C** optional theory window.  
Interactive equation cards also live under **Reference** in the HTML course.

## \(F = m_i a\)

- **A:** Inertia resists pushes.  
- **B:** Acceleration is force per inertial mass.  
- **C:** Four-force / relativistic inertia (optional).  

## Newtonian gravity

- **A:** Sources attract test “gravitational charge.”  
- **B:** \(F_g = -G M m_g / r^2\).  
- **C:** Poisson equation \(\nabla^2\Phi = 4\pi G\rho\) as field form.  

## Mass cancellation

- **B:** \(a = (m_g/m_i) GM/r^2\); equality ⇒ universal \(a\).  
- **Misuse:** treating equality as a definition rather than empirical input.  

## \(E = mc^2\) (as needed)

- Rest energy scale for extractable-energy talk.  
- Not a substitute for full relativistic energy at infinity.  

## Escape velocity (Newtonian clue)

- \(v_{\mathrm{esc}} = \sqrt{2GM/r}\).  
- Setting \(v_{\mathrm{esc}}=c\) gives \(r = 2GM/c^2\) as a **scale clue only**.  

## Schwarzschild radius

- \(r_s = 2GM/c^2\).  
- Domain notes for horizon vs exterior static observers.  

## Static redshift / time factor

- \(\sqrt{1 - r_s/r}\) for **static** observers, \(r > r_s\).  
- **Misuse:** applying to free-fall horizon crossing.  

## Idealized extractable fraction

- \(1 - \sqrt{1 - r_s/r}\) under quasi-static lowering assumptions.  

## Tidal approximation

- \(\Delta a \approx 2 G M L / r^3\).  
- Larger holes → weaker horizon tides.  

## Weak-field light deflection

- \(\alpha \approx 4GM/(b c^2)\).  
- Not a full lensing solver.  

## Orbital mass inference

- \(M \approx 4\pi^2 a^3 / (G T^2)\) (simplified Kepler).  
- Constrains mass; does not prove every horizon property.  

## Einstein field equation

- \(G_{\mu\nu} = (8\pi G/c^4) T_{\mu\nu}\).  
- Interpret sides; do not claim arbitrary exact solutions.
