# Exercises

Interactive versions with feedback are in each lesson’s Practice section and in `practice/index.html`.

## Per-lesson pattern

1. Two prediction prompts before explanation  
2. Draw-it-yourself board task  
3. One thought experiment  
4. Three conceptual checks  
5. Algebra when appropriate  
6. Misconception diagnosis  
7. Teach-back  

## Lesson 1 highlights

**Prediction:** 1 kg vs 10 kg in vacuum — who accelerates faster?  
**Draw:** \(F=m_i a\), gravity with \(m_g\), equality, cancellation, one sentence on charge.  
**Teach-back:** surprise of \(m_i=m_g\) vs electric charge.  

## Lesson 2 highlights

**Thought experiment:** rocket vs Earth elevator vs free fall.  
**Check:** why tides limit equivalence.  

## Lesson 3 highlights

**Draw:** flat-map straight line vs great circle.  
**Check:** graph curvature ≠ spacetime curvature.  

## Lesson 4 highlights

**Interpret:** both sides of Einstein’s equation; what \(T_{\mu\nu}\) includes.  

## Lesson 5 highlights

**Algebra:** compute \(r_s\) for Sun and for \(10 M_\odot\).  
**Check:** horizon vs shell.  

## Lesson 6 highlights

**Reconcile:** distant fading vs finite proper-time crossing.  
**Algebra:** static factor at \(r=2r_s\); tidal scaling with \(M\).  

## Lesson 7 highlights

**Transfer:** what orbits prove vs what EHT shows vs what GW add.
