# Essence of GR — Course Outline

Canonical interactive delivery: open `index.html` via `npm run dev` or the `dist/` build.

This Markdown outline mirrors the seven lessons for print/export and tutoring.

## Lesson 1 — The Coincidence Einstein Took Seriously

**Insight:** All bodies fall alike because gravity couples universally.

- SR speed-of-light constraint  
- \(F = m_i a\) vs Newtonian \(m_g\)  
- Coulomb comparison  
- Cancellation only after empirical equality  
- **App:** Mass Cancellation Lab  

## Lesson 2 — Einstein’s Happiest Thought

**Insight:** Locally, free fall removes gravity; standing on Earth is accelerated.

- Real vs inertial forces  
- Elevator / rocket equivalence (local)  
- Tides break global equivalence  
- **App:** Einstein Elevator  

## Lesson 3 — Gravity Becomes Geometry

**Insight:** Matter changes what natural straight motion is.

- Worldlines  
- Map/globe geodesics (analogy)  
- Avoid literal rubber-sheet spacetime  
- **Apps:** Worldline Lab; Map-to-Globe  

## Lesson 4 — Field Equation Without Tensor Calculus

**Insight:** Feedback law between stress-energy and geometry.

- \(G_{\mu\nu} = (8\pi G/c^4) T_{\mu\nu}\) interpreted  
- Pressure/stress gravitate  
- **App:** Field Equation Relationship Explorer (conceptual only)  

## Lesson 5 — Why a Black Hole Must Be Black

**Insight:** Horizon is a causal boundary.

- Newtonian escape clue ≠ GR proof  
- \(r_s = 2GM/c^2\)  
- Brick-and-pulley energy accounting  
- **Apps:** Schwarzschild Scale; Brick Energy  

## Lesson 6 — Clocks, Energy, Horizon Crossing

**Insight:** Distant and infalling stories differ without contradiction.

- Static redshift factor domain  
- Dual observers  
- Tides vs black-hole mass  
- Horizon ≠ singularity  
- **Apps:** Clock/Redshift; Dual Observer; Tidal Force  

## Lesson 7 — How Nature Answers Back

**Insight:** Independent empirical channels.

- Sgr A* orbits  
- Gravitational waves  
- EHT (not a solid-horizon photo)  
- Light bending / 1919  
- **Apps:** Orbit Inference; Light Bending
